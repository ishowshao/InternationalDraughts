# Privacy Policy

International Draughts does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

The app can use Apple's local network and nearby peer discovery features when
you create or join a nearby two-player match. This is only used to find another
device near you and exchange game moves for that match. The app does not send
game data to a server and does not include advertising, analytics, account
registration, or third-party tracking SDKs.

If you choose not to grant local network permission, you can still play local
games against the built-in AI opponent.

For support, open an issue at:
https://github.com/ishowshao/InternationalDraughts/issues
